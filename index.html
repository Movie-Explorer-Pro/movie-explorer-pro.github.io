Discover Movie Explorer Pro

Movie Explorer Pro is the most comfortable way to catalog and explore your digital movie files, TV shows, Blu-rays, 4K UHD discs, and DVDs. Index your collection once, then effortlessly browse stunning thumbnails, original posters, and high-res stills. Track watched status, manage watchlists, and create smart filters tailored to your tastes.

Effortless Collection Building

Index local drives and network shares for movies and TV shows. Scan barcodes with FaceTime camera, external USB scanners, or even iPhone/external webcams. Batch process for efficiency. Full support for discs and episodes.

Precise Tracking and Discovery

Mark items as watched, unwatched, or paused. Rate and review with personal filters. Build watchlists that auto-update. Sync status with Trakt.tv. Search by actor, director, or custom queries.

Rich Exploration Tools

Gallery view for posters and stills. Detailed Info view with metadata, cast, and crew. Files view reveals video format, bitrate, audio channels. Quick IMDb links and 'Play with' for any media player.

Customizable and Shareable

Adjust thumbnails, sorting, and sidebar filters. Create Queries for complex criteria and Lists for collections. Export to PDF, HTML, or CSV. Lend discs and track loans. Native macOS design with Dark Mode, Touch Bar, and keyboard navigation.

What's New in 2.8

Batch refresh multiple file locations. Drag-and-drop custom disc covers. External webcam/iPhone barcode scanning. Updated app icon with macOS 26 styling support.

How It Works

Step 1: Build Your Library

Index drives, scan barcodes, or drag files. Supports movies, TV seasons, discs—everything in batch mode.

Step 2: Organize and Track

Mark watched status, rate items, add to watchlists. Sync with Trakt.tv for seamless progress.

Step 3: Explore and Filter

Use sidebar filters, custom Queries, or search. Browse galleries, metadata, and play directly.

Step 4: Share and Export

Generate PDFs/HTML for your site, export CSV data, or share via Mail/Messages. Track lent discs effortlessly.
